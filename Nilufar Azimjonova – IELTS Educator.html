Student Learning Segmentation (AI and ML capstone defense)

My defense project for the AI and ML course. It helps schools notice students who are quietly losing engagement before it shows up in their grades. Instead of sending every student the same message, advisors get four clear student profiles, each with a suggested way to support them, using data the platform already collects. It was tested on students it had never seen, checked for fairness, and is honest about the cases it is unsure of. Try the live demo to see how one student, or a whole class, would be grouped.
